The hypothesis

H1 (primary).

When the canonical ARSPI-Net LIF reservoir is driven by NMED-T EEG, its beat-locked spiking is not fully explained by an evoked-superposition model. After you subtract a cross-validated forward mTRF prediction from the audio, the EEG residual still drives beat-phase-locked reservoir activity above a circular-shift null. Across the ten song tempi, the beat-locked phase lag is flatter than the lag the evoked twin produces.

H1b (does ARSPI-Net add value?): beat-cycle BSC6 features separate real EEG drive from its evoked twin better than linear beat-cycle features (raw 6-bin beat-cycle ERP, steady-state amplitude at the beat frequency) under participant-grouped cross-validation. If H1b fails, the reservoir is only acting as a filter for this question, and later weeks should use raw or mTRF features.

Scope of the claim: a positive result shows beat-locked structure beyond linear stimulus-driven superposition. It does not by itself prove a self-sustained oscillator. Nonlinear or anticipatory evoked activity (for example the CNV) is a live alternative, covered under Methodological challenges.

Why NMED-T over DEAP or OpenMIIR for Week 1: ten tempi on a steady electronic grid give you a built-in rate manipulation for the phase-lag test. Each listen is continuous, so there is no trial averaging to hide oscillatory dynamics. Your BIBM repo already handles DEAP (analysis/deap_replication.py, 32 channels at 128 Hz), but DEAP's 1-minute music videos have no tempo design.

What the reservoir adds beyond frequency tagging and mTRF
Frequency tagging gives power at the beat frequency, which superposed ERPs also produce (Novembre & Iannetti). The reservoir's thresholded, recurrent transform responds to phase structure that amplitude spectra ignore. Your Exp 6.3 found τ_AC and LZ separate real from phase-randomized drive.
An mTRF is linear by construction, so whatever it cannot predict lands in the residual. Driving a fixed nonlinear operator with that residual tests whether the residual holds organized beat-locked structure, not just noise.
The instrument is calibrated. Its group delay and gain per frequency can be measured with synthetic drives and subtracted, so its own filtering is not mistaken for brain latency.
The readout is traceable. Beat-cycle BSC6 shows which reservoir neurons are phase-tuned, at which beat phase, and with what membrane time scale. That neuron-level account is the Level 1/3 traceability your dissertation built.
Your destruction controls (shuffling bin order, collapsing bins) carry over directly to beat-phase bins.

1

Stimulus features

Compute the amplitude envelope at 256 Hz, half-wave-rectified envelope derivative (onsets), and beat times from a beat tracker. Accept a song only if the tracked tempo is within 1% of Table 1 and the tapping medians agree.

2

EEG preparation

Use scipy.signal.resample_poly(x, 256, 125) so β = 0.05 keeps the 76 ms meaning it has in SHAPE. Z-score per channel per song, as in preprocess_epoch. Drop the first 10 s (onset transient). Pre-register a fronto-central cluster of about 16 electrodes plus one PCA/RCA component as drives.

3

Evoked model

Per subject, fit a forward TRF (lags −100 to 500 ms, ridge λ by inner CV) from envelope and onsets to each channel. Train on 9 songs and predict the held-out song.

4

Two drives per song

Real = the actual EEG, i.e. TRF prediction plus the true residual. Evoked twin = TRF prediction plus the phase-randomized residual (phase_randomize). Spectra match; only beat-locked residual structure differs.

5

Run the reservoir

Drive LIFReservoir(seed=s + ch*17) for s in (7, 42, 99). Keep spikes and membrane. A 60 s drive takes about 0.2 s on the box, so the full 20 × 10 × 17-drive pilot is minutes.

6

Beat-phase readout

Assign each step a beat phase. Bin population rate and per-neuron counts into 6 phase bins (a beat-cycle BSC6). Compute Tort's modulation index MI per drive, the fraction of phase-tuned neurons, and Ch6 descriptors (τ_AC, LZ) per song.

7

Cross-tempo phase test

Per subject, regress the unwrapped beat-locked phase of the population rate on tempo (10 songs). The slope is an apparent latency. Subtract the reservoir's own slope, calibrated with a synthetic phase-locked oscillator, then compare real vs twin slopes.

8

Group statistics

Subject-level z-scores against 1,000 circular shifts. Use Wilcoxon / paired tests across 20 subjects, participant-cluster bootstrap CIs (participant_bootstrap_ci), and equivalence bounds fixed in the pre-registration.

Fix the equivalence bounds before unblinding: for example ±20 ms apparent latency and ±0.2 in subject-level MI z-score. With n = 20, a paired test has about 80% power for d_z ≈ 0.66. That is the smallest effect the pilot can claim to detect.

Key assumptions
The purchased audio matches the files used in the study and aligns with EEG onsets (onsets were click-corrected). Verify with envelope–EEG cross-correlation.
Steady electronic beats make a fixed beat grid valid. Perceived beat may still sit at a metrical level other than the listed tempo; tapping data checks this.
Resampling to 256 Hz keeps the reservoir in the regime characterized on SHAPE. Firing rate and λ₁ must be re-checked under continuous drive.
A linear TRF with envelope and onset regressors captures most of the evoked response. Anything it misses counts as 'beyond superposition', which is why the claim is scoped.
Group-level beat locking in single-listen EEG is detectable at n = 20. Losorelli et al. show beat-frequency peaks in PCA-filtered averages; single-subject reliability is unknown.

Toward consciousness and meditative states

If H1 holds, the beat-phase MI and the residual-driven descriptors (τ_AC, LZ) form an index of entrainment depth that separates stimulus-driven tracking from internally sustained timing. The arc from there:

Week 2–3: test whether that index tracks enjoyment or familiarity ratings within NMED-T, which bridges entrainment and experience.
Next: drive the same calibrated reservoir with EEG from rhythmic practices (mantra, breath-paced or drone listening vs silent sitting) to ask whether absorptive states show more internally sustained, less stimulus-explained beat-locking.
Longer term: relate reservoir complexity descriptors to state reports, while keeping your measurement-instrument framing. These are functionals of the driven reservoir, not direct readouts of consciousness.
