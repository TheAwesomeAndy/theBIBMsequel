The lateralized N2pc, whose condition difference reverses sign across hemispheres, also leaks across participants (0.597, $p=0.0010$, Holm-adjusted 0.003), because each channel's epoch mean carries its own signed contrast (39 participants: one has no retained trial in one condition). The MMN gives the weakest effect: 0.553 under per-epoch z-scoring ($p=0.055$ with 2000 permutations, 0.045 with 200) against 0.537 unscaled ($p=0.088$), and no within-participant leak. Its within-participant statistic, 0.499, lies 0.001 below chance with a small standard error, the slight negative bias of cross-validated accuracy on a code without class information \cite{jamalabadi2016below}.
